## 1. Zero Membership & Zero Personal Data Collection (Sıfır Üyelik & Sıfır Veri Toplama)
**AstroVibe** is engineered with a strict **Zero-Membership Architecture**:
- **No User Accounts:** You never need to register, create an account, or provide personal credentials.
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, process, or transmit your name, phone number, location, contacts, or device identifiers.
- **Instant Usability:** The app is 100% functional immediately upon launch.

---

## 2. On-Device Local Cache Architecture (Cihaz İçi Yerel Önbellek)
- All user preferences, history, and records in **AstroVibe** are stored strictly on your local device.
- Your data never leaves your physical device and is never synchronized to cloud profiling databases.
- You can clear all cached data at any time via the in-app "Clear Cache" button or your OS app settings.
