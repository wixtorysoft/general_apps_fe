**Scope:** Applies to all mobile applications, web tools, and software published under the Wixtory brand, including **Wixtory: Domain Track**, **Wixtory: Language Box**, **AstroVibe**, **Excuse AI**, and **all upcoming new applications and software releases** across our digital ecosystem.

## 1. Zero Membership System & Zero Personal Data Collection (Sıfır Üyelik & Sıfır Veri)

We believe fundamentally and unconditionally in user privacy. All current applications—including **Wixtory: Domain Track**, **Wixtory: Language Box**, **AstroVibe**, **Excuse AI**—as well as **all upcoming projects** released by Wixtory, are engineered with a strict **Zero-Membership Architecture**:

- **No User Accounts:** You never need to register, create a username, provide an email address, or enter a password.
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, harvest, process, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, phone number, location, contacts, device IMEI, or national identifiers.
- **Instant Usability:** Every app is completely functional the instant you open it, with zero onboarding forms or credential requests.

---

## 2. On-Device Local Cache Architecture (Cihaz İçi Önbellek Sistemi)

To deliver an ultra-fast, responsive, and offline-first user experience, our applications utilize the physical device's native local storage and client-side caching mechanisms rather than centralized server databases:

- **Storage Technologies Used:** Secure on-device storage engines (`SharedPreferences` on Android, `NSUserDefaults` on iOS, client-side `SQLite`, and web `LocalStorage`).
- **Application-Specific Local Cache Breakdown:**
  - **Wixtory: Domain Track:** Domain search history, bookmarked favorite domains, extension filter preferences, and local query counters are saved strictly in your device's local cache.
  - **Wixtory: Language Box:** Game progress across Sentence Builder, Word Matrix, Word Compass, and Scrambled Words, current question indices, difficulty levels, score records, and study streaks reside entirely on your device.
  - **AstroVibe:** Saved nail art inspirations, jewelry favorites, curated baby names, zodiac birth sign selections, and tarot card draws are preserved exclusively in device memory.
  - **Excuse AI:** Generated excuses, emergency crisis scenarios, and favorited categories are saved in the app's local offline database.
  - **Upcoming & Future Projects (Yeni Eklenecek Projeler):** Any upcoming apps joining the Wixtory ecosystem automatically adhere to this exact same on-device caching design. All user preferences, logs, and progress will remain 100% on the device without server-side user profiling.
- **Data Location & Absolute Ownership:** Your data never leaves your device. It is never synchronized to cloud servers, external data brokers, or linked to your identity.
- **User Control & One-Tap "Clear Cache" (Önbelleği Temizle):** You maintain 100% control over your device data. You can completely purge all search history, game progress, favorite bookmarks, and cache files at any moment using the in-app **"Clear Cache" / "Önbelleği Temizle"** button or via your device's operating system application settings.

## 3. Search Statistics & Offline Capability

Our applications may display summarized personal metrics (e.g., number of domains checked, words learned, favorite items saved):

- These calculations and counters are executed 100% client-side on your device's hardware processor.
- They are **never** transmitted to remote analytics servers or third-party tracking services.
- The majority of features and cached history remain completely accessible even when your device is completely offline or in airplane mode.

---

## 4. Device Permissions & Hardware Access Policy (Cihaz İzinleri ve Donanım Erişimi)

None of the applications in the Wixtory ecosystem request or require sensitive device permissions:

- **No Sensitive Access:** We do not request access to GPS Location, Contacts, Camera, Microphone, SMS/Call logs, or Media Storage.
- **Network Permissions:** Our applications rely solely on standard baseline network permissions (`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `INTERNET`) to verify connectivity and communicate with our encrypted content endpoints.
- **Local Notifications:** If you choose to enable study or horoscope reminders, standard on-device local notification permissions are used. We never inspect or scan your device's private hardware or personal media files.

## 5. AI & Algorithmic Processing Transparency (Yapay Zeka & Algoritmik Şeffaflık)

For smart features across Excuse AI (creative excuse generation), AstroVibe (tarot and astrology matching logic), and Language Box (adaptive vocabulary difficulty):

- **Ephemeral Processing:** All inputs, prompts, and queries are processed ephemerally in real-time.
- **Zero Model Training:** Your inputs, scenarios, and queries are **never** stored permanently on remote servers, never linked to personal profiles, and **never** used to train machine learning or AI models.
- **Zero Commercial Harvesting:** All algorithmic outputs are designed strictly for entertainment, educational progress, and creative inspiration without any hidden user behavioral profiling.

## 6. Real-Time Network Communication & APIs (HTTPS & TLS 1.3)

When real-time network requests are required (e.g., domain availability queries in Wixtory: Domain Track, or downloading static design catalogs in AstroVibe & Excuse AI):

- All communications are encrypted using modern industry-standard **HTTPS / TLS 1.3** protocols.
- Requests contain solely the specific query (e.g., domain name or catalog ID); **no** personal identity tokens, hardware serial numbers, or advertising profiles are transmitted or logged.

## 7. Third-Party Advertising & Monetization (Google AdMob)

To maintain all Wixtory applications as free-to-download software without paywalls or subscriptions, applications may integrate third-party advertisements via Google AdMob:

- Ad networks may utilize standard anonymous mobile advertising identifiers (IDFA on Apple iOS, AAID / Google Advertising ID on Google Android) governed by Google's Privacy Policies.
- Users can disable personalized ads or reset their advertising ID at any time directly through their device settings:
  - **iOS:** Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  - **Android:** Settings > Privacy > Ads

## 8. Under-18 Policy (Child & Teen Privacy - COPPA & GDPR-K)

Wixtory applications are designed with safety and privacy by default:

- We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data from children or individuals under 18 years of age.
- Because our applications operate on a strict zero-account and zero-PII architecture, minors cannot be profiled, tracked, or exposed through our platforms.
- Parents and guardians can clear local device caches or reset preferences at any time through operating system settings.

---

## 9. Data Retention & App Deletion / Erasure (Veri Saklama Süresi ve Uygulama Kaldırma)

- **Zero Server Retention:** Because we do not maintain user accounts or identity registries on centralized servers, server-side personal data retention is **ZERO (0 days)**.
- **Automatic Purge on Uninstall:** All locally stored search queries, vocabulary skills, and bookmark preferences exist solely on your physical device for as long as you keep the app installed.
- Uninstalling the application from your operating system (iOS / Android) immediately and permanently purges all local databases, cache files, and preferences without leaving trace files behind.
- You can also manually reset all local data at any time via the "Clear Cache" button in the app settings.

- In compliance with GDPR Chapter V and KVKK Article 9, Wixtory does **not** transfer personal data, identities, or search records across international borders or to third-party data brokers.
